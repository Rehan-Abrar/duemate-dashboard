# DueMate Design System

Version 2.0

---

# Product

## Name

DueMate

## Description

DueMate is an AI-powered academic companion for university students.

It helps students manage their academic life by understanding tasks, tracking deadlines, organizing schedules, and sending smart reminders through WhatsApp.

Core capabilities:

- AI task understanding
- WhatsApp-first reminders
- Assignment and exam tracking
- Academic productivity dashboard
- AI academic assistant

DueMate is not a task management tool.

It is a personal academic partner that keeps a student's semester organized automatically.

---

# Brand Vision

## Never let a student miss another deadline.

DueMate removes academic stress caused by forgotten assignments, quizzes, exams, presentations, and deadlines.

The experience should feel like:

"An intelligent friend who keeps my semester organized."

Not:

"Another productivity app I have to maintain."

# Design Philosophy

## Modern Neumorphic AI Productivity UI

DueMate follows a modern neumorphic design language.

The experience combines:

- Soft UI depth
- Premium AI product aesthetics
- Minimal productivity design
- Calm academic visuals
- Tactile interactions

The interface should feel:

Soft.

Intelligent.

Reliable.

Effortless.

---

# Visual Inspiration

Inspired by:

- Apple Health simplicity
- Notion Calendar organization
- Linear minimalism
- Superhuman productivity
- Modern AI assistant products

Do not copy existing products.

Create an original DueMate identity.

# Screen Design Rules

Every screen answers one question.

---

# Landing Page

Question:

"What is DueMate and why should I use it?"

Must communicate within 5 seconds:

- What DueMate does
- Who it helps
- Why it is valuable

Prioritize:

- Strong hero message
- Product preview
- AI value
- WhatsApp reminder benefit
- Clear CTA

Avoid:

- Generic SaaS landing pages
- Long explanations

---

# Dashboard

Question:

"What needs my attention?"

Prioritize:

1. Today's Focus
2. Upcoming deadlines
3. AI suggestions
4. Recent activity

Avoid:

- Large tables
- Complex analytics
- Enterprise dashboards

The dashboard should feel like a personal assistant.

---

# Tasks

Question:

"What do I need to complete?"

Prioritize:

- Task cards
- Due dates
- Course
- Priority
- Status

Avoid:

- Spreadsheet layouts
- Project management complexity

---

# Calendar

Question:

"When is everything happening?"

Prioritize:

- Agenda
- Timeline
- Month view

Show:

- Assignments
- Exams
- Presentations

Avoid:

- Overcrowded calendars

---

# AI Assistant

Question:

"How can DueMate help me?"

Should feel like:

A helpful academic companion.

Not:

A customer support chatbot.

Include:

- Conversation
- Suggested prompts
- Quick actions
- Context awareness

---

# Empty States

Every empty state requires:

1. Simple visual
2. Helpful message
3. Clear CTA

Never show blank screens.

---

# Loading

Use:

Skeleton loading.

Avoid unnecessary spinners.

---

# Animation

Duration:

200-300ms

Easing:

Ease Out

Animations should feel:

- Smooth
- Calm
- Helpful

Avoid:

- Flashy motion
- Excessive effects

---

# Accessibility

Requirements:

- WCAG AA contrast
- Readable typography
- Visible focus states
- Minimum button height 48px
- Touch-friendly controls

---

# Final Quality Standard

DueMate should feel like:

A premium AI startup product built for modern university students.

It should never feel like:

- A student project
- A generic productivity app
- A simple reminder tool

The final experience should communicate:

"DueMate understands my academic life and keeps me organized automatically."
