# DueMate Brand Guidelines

Version: 2.0

# Product Identity

## Product

DueMate

AI-powered academic companion for university students.

DueMate helps students manage their academic life by understanding tasks, tracking deadlines, organizing schedules, and delivering smart reminders through WhatsApp.

DueMate combines:

• AI task understanding
• WhatsApp-first reminders
• Academic deadline tracking
• Personal productivity dashboard
• AI academic assistant

DueMate is not just a task manager.

It is a personal academic partner that helps students stay organized automatically.

---

# Brand Vision

## Never let a student miss another deadline.

DueMate exists to remove academic stress caused by forgotten assignments, quizzes, exams, presentations, and deadlines.

The product should feel like:

"An intelligent friend who keeps my semester organized."

Not:

"Another productivity app I have to maintain."

---

# Brand Mission

Make academic organization effortless by combining AI assistance with the communication tools students already use.

# Device Strategy

## Primary Experience

Mobile-first

DueMate is designed primarily for smartphone users because students communicate and manage their daily lives through mobile devices.

## Secondary Experience

Desktop and tablet companion dashboard.

All layouts should scale from mobile while maintaining the same design language.

---

# Design Philosophy

## Modern Neumorphic AI Productivity

DueMate uses a modern neumorphic design language.

The experience should combine:

• Soft UI depth
• Premium AI product aesthetics
• Minimal productivity design
• Calm visual hierarchy
• Tactile interactions

The interface should feel:

Soft.

Intelligent.

Reliable.

Effortless.

---

# Visual Inspiration

Inspired by:

• Apple Health simplicity
• Notion Calendar organization

Create an original DueMate identity.

# Visual Style

## Use

• Soft raised surfaces
• Subtle inset elements
• Gentle shadows
• Rounded cards
• Spacious layouts
• Calm backgrounds
• Clear typography
• Premium whitespace

## Avoid

• Generic SaaS templates
• Bootstrap layouts
• Admin dashboards
• Heavy gradients
• Cartoon illustrations
• Excessive glass effects
• Overly futuristic AI visuals

# Typography Rules

Use:

• Large hero headings
• Clear hierarchy
• Generous line spacing
• High readability

Never use:

• Decorative fonts
• Playful fonts
• Excessive typography styles

# Shadows

Use soft dual shadows.

Light shadow:

White highlight

Dark shadow:

Soft blue-grey depth

Rules:

• Never use harsh shadows
• Never create floating 3D objects
• Maintain calm depth
